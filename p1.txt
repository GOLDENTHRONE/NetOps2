GOAL

I want a robust Applications health model where:

Healthy = we have complete enough evidence that the application is healthy.
Unhealthy = we have authoritative evidence of a failure.
Progressing/Degraded = the application is observable but not fully healthy.
Unknown = we cannot make a trustworthy health judgment because required evidence is missing, loading, forbidden, unreachable, truncated, etc.

The most important principle:

"Absence of evidence must never be interpreted as evidence of health."
